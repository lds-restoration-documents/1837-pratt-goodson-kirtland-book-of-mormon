# Transcription notes — 1837 Pratt & Goodson Kirtland Book of Mormon

Companion to the `book-of-mormon-1837-pages/` folder (one markdown file per image). This file records how the text is produced, the conventions used, and every place where the transcription is doubtful, illegible, or reflects a judgment call. Structure follows `../1830-grandin-palmyra-book-of-mormon/transcription-notes.md`. **Status: project set up; images 1–288 transcribed and audited once (batches 1–18); the rest is not transcribed, audited or proofread.**

## 1. Source and method

- **Source:** 619 JPG images, `source/001.jpg` … `619.jpg` (gitignored), from https://archive.org/details/BookOfMormon1837KirtlandEdition (the images were dated 2014-05-20 in the original `_AllFiles.zip` download, since deleted). Each is about 1160 x 2200 px, grayscale, bitonal-looking, one printed page per image, noisy, with specks and mirror-image bleed-through from the reverse of the leaf. No text layer; no OCR.
- **Layout (image numbers), from a survey of thumbnails and header strips:** 1 title page ("THE BOOK OF MORMON: AN ACCOUNT WRITTEN BY THE HAND OF MORMON … TRANSLATED BY JOSEPH SMITH, Jr. KIRTLAND, OHIO: PRINTED BY O. COWDERY & CO. FOR P. P. PRATT AND J. GOODSON. 1837."); 2 title-page verso (copyright notice, District of New York); 3 Preface (printed v), closing with the signatures of Parley P. Pratt and John Goodson, Kirtland, Ohio, 1837; 4 Preface continued (vi); 5 opening of 1 Nephi (no folio printed, which would be p. 7); 6 onward main text. **Printed folio = image + 2** at every spot checked (images 10, 25, 50, 75, …, 600, 601, 604, 610, 617 = pp. 12, 27, 52, 77, … 602, 603, 606, 612, 619). 618 testimony of the Three Witnesses; 619 testimony of the Eight Witnesses and a notice "To the Reader" signed "The Publishers".
- **Books, with the image where each opens (as transcribed):** 1 Nephi 5; 2 Nephi 62; Jacob mid-129; Enos 151; Jarom 153; Omni 155; Words of Mormon 159; Mosiah 161; Alma 233; Helaman mid-428; Book of Nephi (3 Nephi) 475; 4 Nephi ("THE BOOK OF NEPHI, WHO IS THE SON OF NEPHI…") 540; Book of Mormon 545; Ether mid-566; Moroni 603; Three Witnesses 618; Eight Witnesses, To the Reader and The Publishers 619. The main text ends on image 617. The edition prints chapter numbers both as `CHAPTER 1.` and `CHAPTER II.` and sometimes with no closing period; each is recorded as printed.
- **Not in this edition:** verse numbers, marginal notes, reference letters. It has italic chapter summaries, ordinary punctuation, an ornament between chapters, and a possible thin space before `; : ? !` (not transcribed; see `tools/transcription-spec.md`).
- **Process:** each image is cut by `tools/prepare_images.py` into a 1/3-size overview plus three overlapping bands. An AI model reads the bands and writes the page. The image is authoritative. Pages are processed in batches of 16, skipping pages whose output exists. A pilot of about three pages (5, 10 and a chapter break) is done in the main session first.
- **Models / review chain:** images 5, 10 and 16 (pilot) were written directly in the main session by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from the three bands, with 2x zooms of the source for two doubtful spots; images 1–4, 6–9 and 11–15 by a Sonnet subagent (batch 1); all 16 audited by a second Sonnet subagent. Later batches are recorded here per range, as in the 1830 notes (transcribe, then audit, then blind proofread).
- **Resolution:** upscaling a band gives no new information. Expect more doubtful marks than in the 1830 project because of noise and bleed-through.

## 2. Conventions

See `tools/transcription-spec.md`. In short: one file per image starting `<!-- image NNN -->`; `**Header:**` running head; `#` book titles, `## CHAPTER X.` (numerals as printed: this edition has `CHAPTER 1.` and `CHAPTER II.`); one paragraph per printed paragraph with `<!-- cont -->` marking a paragraph continued from the previous page; `*italic*` as printed; line-end hyphenations joined; spelling, punctuation and printer's errors kept as printed; no space before `; : ? !`; doubtful readings `<!-- uncertain: … -->`, unreadable `[illegible]`; signature marks / catchwords on a `Footer:` line.

## 3. Silent corrections and judgment calls

Carried over from the 1830 project (to be confirmed or revised for this edition as pages are done):

- **Spacing before `; : ? !` is not transcribed.**
- **Specks, dirt lines and bleed-through are ignored.** A mark that does not read as a deliberate mark or letter gets no comment.
- **Page-end hyphens** are kept and the next page starts `<!-- cont -->`.
- **Flush-left versus indented first line:** a flush-left first line continues the previous page's paragraph.
- **Scan-edge clipping:** write the visible fragment; keep a letter if about half or more is visible.
- **Small caps versus ordinary type:** opening words in small caps are written as capitals; a merely capitalized word keeps its case.
- **Comma/period tie-breaker:** see `tools/proofread-spec.md`.
